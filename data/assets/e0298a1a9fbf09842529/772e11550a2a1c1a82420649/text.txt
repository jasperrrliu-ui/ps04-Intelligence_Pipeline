Coming Soon: The Exorcist
Grace Krenz |
October 8, 2026
Based on the novel by William Peter Blatty, Playhouse 615 presents John Leanoard Pielmeier’s 2012 stage adaptation of this classic horror tale. A young girl, Reagan MacNeil, is possessed by a demon, and her mother strives to get her exorcized by Catholic priests.
Please note that this production is 18+ only.
The Exorcist will be at Playhouse 615 October 16-November 1, including a special Halloween showing. Check out their website for tickets and more information.
