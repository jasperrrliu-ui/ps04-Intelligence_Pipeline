Coming Soon: The Curious Savage
Grace Krenz |
October 7, 2026
The Keeton Theatre brings us dinner and a show! When wealthy widow Ethel Savage decides to give away her fortune to strangers rather than her greedy stepchildren, chaos ensues. Placed in a sanatorium by her conniving heirs, Ethel finds unlikely allies in the quirky residents, who help her outsmart her scheming family. This delightful comedy blends heart and humor, celebrating kindness, individuality, and the joy of being unconventional.
The Curious Savage will be at the Keeton October 9-25th. See their website for tickets and more information.
