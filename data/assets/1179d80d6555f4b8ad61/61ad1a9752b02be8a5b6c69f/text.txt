Coming soon: Silence! The Musical
Grace Krenz |
October 7, 2026
Street Theatre brings us this raucous parody of The Silence of the Lambs, F.B.I. trainee Clarice Starling uses her relationship with Dr. Hannibal Lecter to hunt serial killer Buffalo Bill. The film is transformed into a full-scale musical, complete with singing serial killers & a chorus of tap-dancing lambs.
Please note that this production is 18+ only.
Silence! The Musical will be at the Barbershop Theater October 9-24th, see their website for tickets and more information.
