# Editorial policy revision after six-decision sample

The sample includes three accepted items (National Theatre set-design video, LIGHT TALK/Cory Pattak podcast, AuraLuxMuse preprint) and three non-accepted items (ABT video, WICKED stage-management video, Space Hamlet web interview). The first five decisions are defensible with scope or evidence caveats. The Space Hamlet deferral reason is factually wrong: it calls a fetched web article an unverified transcript. Policy v2 distinguishes full-text web interviews from ASR and requires reasons to match material type and date metadata. The original policy and decisions are preserved here. Selection is rerun against the same eligible corpus; briefs and drafts remain provisional until comparison and review.
